# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue or discussion.
Email [s.fidanza@pm.me](mailto:s.fidanza@pm.me) with a description, affected
repository and versions, potential impact, and steps to reproduce. Avoid
including credentials, personal data, or unrelated sensitive information.

Reports will be reviewed by the current contact and handled as confidentially as
practical. Please allow time for assessment and remediation before sharing
details publicly.
